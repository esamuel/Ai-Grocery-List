Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings. The cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

Is my data secure?

Yes! All your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use Firebase's secure infrastructure and comply with industry standards.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes! The app is a Progressive Web App (PWA) and works offline. Changes sync when you're back online.

How do I export my data?

You can export your shopping lists as CSV files from the Settings menu. Access it by clicking the gear icon.

Why do I see ads?

Free users see ads from Google AdSense. This helps us provide free access. Upgrade to a paid plan to remove ads.

How do I share my list with family?

Click the "Family" icon (👥) to add family members by email. They'll immediately see your shared list.

Report a Security Issue

If you discover a security vulnerability, please do NOT post it publicly. Instead, email us at security@aigrocerylists.com with details.

We take security seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure. We'll acknowledge your report within 48 hours.

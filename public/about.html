About AI Grocery List

Your smart shopping companion powered by artificial intelligence

Our Mission

AI Grocery List is designed to make grocery shopping faster, smarter, and more affordable. We believe that shopping should be effortless, and spending should be transparent. Using artificial intelligence and smart analytics, we help millions of shoppers organize their purchases and save money.

What We Offer

🤖 AI-Powered Categorization

Our advanced AI automatically organizes your shopping items into relevant categories, saving you time and ensuring nothing gets forgotten.

📊 Spending Insights

Track your grocery spending in real-time with detailed analytics. Understand where your money goes and identify opportunities to save.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Sharing

Collaborate with family members on shared shopping lists. Stay synced and coordinate your purchases across devices in real-time.

🌍 Multi-Language Support

Use the app in English, Hebrew, Spanish, and more. Enjoy a seamless experience in your preferred language.

🎤 Voice Input

Add items using voice commands. Perfect for hands-free shopping while you're at the store or preparing meals.

💾 Smart Sync

Your data syncs instantly across all your devices. Access your list from phone, tablet, or desktop.

Our Technology

AI Grocery List is built on cutting-edge technology including:

  • Google Gemini AI: Powers our intelligent item categorization and suggestions
  • Firebase & Firestore: Ensures secure, real-time data synchronization
  • Modern Web Technologies: React, TypeScript, and Vite for a fast, responsive experience
  • Progressive Web App (PWA): Works online and offline with app-like performance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2024

AI Grocery List ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AI Grocery List application. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and authentication credentials (via Firebase Auth)
  • Shopping Data: Items you add to your list, prices, store locations, and purchase history
  • Family Information: Email addresses of family members you add to your shared list

Usage and Analytics Data

  • PostHog Analytics: We use PostHog to track app usage, feature engagement, and performance metrics
  • TikTok Pixel: We use TikTok's analytics pixel to measure marketing effectiveness and user behavior
  • Device Information: Browser type, OS, device type, and general location (country-level)
  • Log Data: IP address, access timestamps, and pages/features accessed

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

  • Google AdSense: Uses cookies to display personalized ads and measure ad performance
  • Third-Party Cookies: Our advertising partners may set cookies on your device
  • Local Storage: We use browser local storage for app preferences and cached data

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To provide app functionality, sync data across devices, and manage your account
  • Family Sharing: To enable shared lists and family collaboration features
  • Analytics: To understand usage patterns and improve features
  • Advertising: To deliver targeted ads through Google AdSense (for free users)
  • Communication: To send important service updates and respond to support requests
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

3. Google AdSense and Advertising

Our app displays Google AdSense advertisements to free users. Please note:

  • Google AdSense uses cookies to serve personalized ads based on your interests
  • Google may display ads based on your browsing history across the web
  • You can manage your ad preferences at Google Ads Settings
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info
  • Paid subscription users do not see ads

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect your information:

  • Firebase Authentication: Handles secure user authentication
  • Firestore Database: Stores your lists and data securely
  • Google Analytics via PostHog: Tracks app performance and user behavior
  • TikTok Pixel: Measures conversion and user behavior for marketing purposes
  • Stripe/PayPal: Processes subscription payments (does not store credit cards)

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them at:

  • Google Firebase Privacy
  • Google AdSense Policies
  • TikTok Privacy Policy

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Firebase security rules for database access control
  • Regular security audits and updates

However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Data Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

If you are located in the European Union or California, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of marketing communications and analytics
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at: support@aigrocerylists.com

7. Retention of Data

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support. Some data may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information and terminate the child's account.

9. Opt-Out Options

You can control data collection in the following ways:

  • Use your browser's "Do Not Track" feature (we respect DNT signals)
  • Manage cookie preferences in your browser settings
  • Opt out of Google AdSense personalization via Google Ads Settings
  • Disable analytics in the app settings

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the app constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please contact us:

  • Email: support@aigrocerylists.com
  • Response Time: We aim to respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days

This privacy policy is effective as of November 2024 and complies with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable privacy regulations.
